        When I began this project, I expected to determine that the implementation of solar systems had a positive effect on local socioeconomic states in Central Africa. 
        However, I soon found that its effect on socioeconomic states was more complex and confounded than a simple scalar boost. \par
        {\centering\textbf{Definitions}\par}
        \textbf{Photovoltaics:} Materials that convert solar energy into electrical energy \par
        \textbf{Central Africa:} A subregion of the African continent centered primarily around the Congo Basin \par
        \textbf{Development:} Measured as the score on the inequality-adjusted human development index \par
        \textbf{Solar Resource:} the total amount of electromagnetic radiation emitted by the sun that reaches a specific geographic area on Earth
